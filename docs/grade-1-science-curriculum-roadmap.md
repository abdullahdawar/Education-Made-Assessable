# Grade 1 Science curriculum roadmap

The target is Cambridge Primary Science 0097 Stage 1. This project proposes eight chapters. The sequence is a local teaching plan. The two-page Stage 1 extract lists 35 objectives across Thinking and Working Scientifically (8), Biology (7), Chemistry (5), Physics (7), Earth and Space (4), and Science in Context (4). [Cambridge subject page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-primary/curriculum/science/) · [Cambridge teaching guidance](https://cambridge-community.org.uk/guide-to/cambridge-primary/science/) · [Cambridge-authored Stage 1 framework extract](https://www.pea.ae/linkfiles/Science_Primary_Framework/Stage1.pdf)

## Proposed chapters

| No. | Chapter | Teaching progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Living things and their needs | Observe familiar examples, compare their features, and explain how people and other animals meet basic needs. |
| 2 | Flowering plants | Label parts, observe a plant over time, and use repeated observations to discuss its needs. |
| 3 | Our bodies and senses | Name external parts, match sensory information to body parts, and compare people respectfully. |
| 4 | Objects and materials | Separate objects from materials, sort by observable properties, and describe physical changes. |
| 5 | Movement, pushes and pulls | Describe motion and explore pushes and pulls with a toy vehicle. Compare travel using equal-length blocks. |
| 6 | Floating and sinking | Ask a question, predict, observe objects in water, and compare findings with predictions. |
| 7 | Sounds, electricity and magnets | Trace familiar sounds to sources, compare distances, and explore everyday uses of electricity and magnets through separate activities. |
| 8 | Earth, land and the Sun | Read a globe, compare rock and soil, and connect sunlight to heat and light. Discuss how evidence changed ideas about Earth. |

## Safety and review gates

Use adult-selected materials and supervised practical work. Keep electricity examples picture-based or use intact battery-powered objects. Avoid mains equipment, loose or small magnets, broken glass, tasting, loud sounds and direct viewing of the Sun. Educator and fluent Standard Written Pashto review are pending.
