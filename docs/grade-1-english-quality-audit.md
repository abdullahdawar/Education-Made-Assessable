## Phonics and handwriting review

Checked grapheme and word boundaries against the DfE guidance independently of configured answer strings. *Map* and *sun* each use three single-letter spellings. *Ship/shop* begin with one consonant digraph while *slip* has /s/ followed by /l/. *Seed* contains `ee`, *night* contains `igh`, and *light* and *night* rhyme from the vowel onward. *Boxes* adds `es`; *cups* adds `s`; *tapped* doubles `p` before `ed`; *resting* adds `ing` to *rest*. *Said* is taught explicitly as a common exception. The first short text uses CVC words and pre-teaches the sentence-initial *A*. Teach the relevant spellings before the Chapter 2 practice lines. The later comprehension texts serve different purposes and may require supported reading. Select a systematic phonics programme with an educator before relying on any set of excerpts for independent decoding.
