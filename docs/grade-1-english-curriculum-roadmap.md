# Grade 1 English curriculum roadmap

This is Education Made Assessable’s teaching sequence. The target is **Cambridge Primary English 0058 Stage 1**. Cambridge identifies 0058 as its first-language English framework. Draft Standard Written Pashto support provides access to instructions while literacy targets remain in English. The 0057 second-language curriculum is a separate programme. [Cambridge subject page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-primary/curriculum/english/)

The inventory was checked against the complete Stage 1 section, pp. 15–21, of the Cambridge-authored June 2025 v2.1 framework hosted as a public copy. Its 90 objectives comprise 43 reading, 29 writing and 18 speaking and listening objectives. Asterisks identify objectives that recur across stages. Descriptions are paraphrased. [Framework](https://resources.pastpapersacademy.com/resources/curriculum/cambridge/0058-English-Curriculum-Framework.pdf) · [Cambridge teaching guidance](https://cambridge-community.org.uk/guide-to/cambridge-primary/english/)

## Sequence and assessment

Adapt the early lessons after checking prior experience. Teach handwriting alongside reading. Progress from simple sound and letter work to varied texts, independent writing and conversation, while spiralling reading aloud and speaking throughout. Use printed books as well as the web. Digital stars show practice completion. Oral, listening, handwritten and group outcomes require direct observation.

| Chapter | Title | Suggested evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Letters, sounds and handwriting | Observe letter names, authentic phonemes, decoding and formation. |
| 2 | Sound teams, blends and rhymes | Check sound-to-spelling, clusters and rhymes with an adult. |
| 3 | Common words, verbs and plurals | Sample meaningful reading and spelling of suffixes and common words. |
| 4 | Picture clues and describing words | Ask the learner to use a picture clue and select precise describing words. |
| 5 | Read together, read aloud | Listen to repeated and independent readings, pausing and rhythm. |
| 6 | Making sentences | Collect independent sentence writing and check punctuation and word order. |
| 7 | Telling and writing stories | Collect an oral plan, retelling, captions and an original story or poem. |
| 8 | Books, instructions and information | Use real books and contrasting texts. Collect appropriate written features. |
| 9 | Read, infer and explain | Collect oral answers, predictions, evidence and independently written notes. |
| 10 | Reading choices and writer reflection | Observe text choice, personal connections and rereading of original work. |
| 11 | Listen, ask and discuss | Give unseen oral instructions. Observe information, questions and group turns. |
| 12 | Perform, speak and reflect | Observe role-play, personal statements and discussion of feelings. |
