# Grade 1 Mathematics curriculum verification

The Counting lesson and game practice Cambridge Primary Mathematics 0096 Stage 1 objectives **1Nc.01** and **1Np.01**. Shape Sorter has a plausible match to **1Gg.01** and remains available through the development review route pending educator approval of the activity and its placement.

| Objective | Cambridge Stage 1 scope | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1Nc.01 | Count objects from zero to twenty, matching one number to each object and recognising that rearrangement preserves quantity. | The lesson models touching or crossing off objects once. Counting Adventure uses collections up to 20, one-at-a-time highlighting and four rearrangements of the same objects. Worksheet 7 and test 3 ask for a checking strategy. The game records choices and does not establish how a learner counted. |
| 1Np.01 | Understand zero as the absence of objects. | The empty tray asks for a total. Worksheet 1 starts with an empty box, worksheet 5 removes every mark, and tests 1 and 2 revisit these contexts. |
| 1Gg.01 | Identify, describe and sort 2D shapes by features such as side count and curved or straight edges. | Shape Sorter lets learners place circles, triangles, squares and non-square rectangle examples in named bins. It supplies descriptions and asks learners to sort. It does not elicit the learner's own description. |

Before placing Shape Sorter in the curriculum, have a Stage 1 educator confirm the placement, language, and the intended classification rule. The rectangle bin shows non-square examples and the square bin is separate for this game. A square is also a rectangle mathematically. The bin wording and feedback should explain the exercise's categories without implying otherwise. Tilted examples support recognition, but selecting the same shape at a different orientation does not by itself assess rotational symmetry or cover the broader Stage 2 objective 2Gg.01. Keep the review route development-only until signoff.
